Decider – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

Decider does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your wheels, spin history, names and settings are saved only in your device’s local app storage so they are still there next time. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Network access

Everything works offline, and fonts and all other resources are bundled with the app. The one request the app can make is the optional update check: if “Check for updates” is on (or you tap “Check for updates now”), it asks GitHub for the latest release of Decider. That request carries no identifier, no account and no device information beyond what any web request includes, and nothing is sent back. Turn the switch off and the app never contacts the network.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the wheel code or result image is handed to your device’s share sheet (or copied to your clipboard). What happens next depends on the app you choose; Decider does not see or receive it.

Device features

Decider may use vibration (haptics), sound, and a “keep screen on” option while it is open. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

Because Decider collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
